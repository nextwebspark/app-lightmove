Browser extension · Chrome side panel

UNCAVA Capture

The side panel, drawn at 400 × 640 and always dark. The position is the panel's context, chosen at the top before anything else and kept until changed. Below it, the LinkedIn profile or company page the consultant is on: a person is filed with the workspace's own pipeline status, its tags and a note; a company goes into the universe with a note. The note is the app's own composer — kinds, formatting, @mentions, who reads it. Once filed, the panel stays on the record: the position's notes on that person or company, added to, edited, pinned and deleted with Undo.
