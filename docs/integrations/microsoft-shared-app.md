# Approve Uncava for your Microsoft 365 organisation

Many organisations let only administrators approve apps that read mail. If your consultants see **"Need admin
approval"** when they connect Outlook, an administrator approves Uncava once for everyone.

1. Sign in to Uncava as a workspace admin and open **Settings → Integrations → Microsoft 365**, with **Shared app**
   chosen.
2. Copy the **Admin consent link** and open it — or send it to whoever administers your Microsoft 365.
3. Sign in with an account holding the **Global Administrator**, **Cloud Application Administrator** or
   **Application Administrator** role.
4. Review the permissions — send email as the user, read and write their mail and calendars, keep access — and select
   **Accept**.
5. Microsoft returns you to Uncava, and the card says **Approved for your organisation**.

Consultants can now connect their mailboxes without asking. Approval does not connect anyone's mailbox by itself, and
you can withdraw it at any time under **Entra admin center → Enterprise applications → Uncava**.

**What Uncava does with it:** sends the emails your consultants write, from their own mailbox; notices who replied,
never what they wrote; books calls and shows meetings with candidates on their calendar.
