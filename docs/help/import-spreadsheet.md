Bring a long list you already have into a position with **Import** on the companies toolbar.

1. Choose a CSV or Excel file. Each row is a person at a company, or a company on its own.
2. Uncava matches your columns to its fields. Check the matches, and change any that are wrong. A column nothing matches becomes a column of its own on this position.
3. Review what will be added, then confirm. Nothing is written until you do.

Download the template from the import dialog to skip the matching step entirely.

Imported rows keep the figures your file states. Uncava doesn't look them up again.
