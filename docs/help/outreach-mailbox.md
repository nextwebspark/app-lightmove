**Outreach** sends email from your own mailbox, so replies come to you.

1. **Connect your mailbox** on a position's **Outreach** page. You sign in with your email provider; Uncava never sees your password.
2. Build a **sequence** of up to three emails. Each person's first email gets its own opening line, which you can review before anything goes.
3. **Add to sequence** from In universe, Shortlisted or an executive's drawer. Uncava shows who it will skip and why — no email, do not contact, or already in a sequence.

Emails go out within your sending window and daily limit. A reply, a bounce or a booked call stops that person's sequence.
