Your first map takes about thirty minutes. The **Get your first map** card on My positions walks you through it, and ticks each step off as the work is done.

1. **Open a position.** Press **New position**, name the role and who you're hiring for.
2. **Write the brief.** Fill in the role, or attach the job description and let Uncava fill it in. Every field saves as you type.
3. **Find target companies.** On **Strategy**, filter the market by industry, size and location, and add the companies you want to the position.
4. **Map executives.** On **In universe**, use **Find executives**, Uncava Capture in Chrome, or **Import** a spreadsheet.
5. **Reach out.** Connect your mailbox on **Outreach**, then add executives to a sequence.
6. **Invite a colleague** from **Team**, so the search is shared.

Put the card away with **I know my way around**. You can bring it back from Help at any time.
