**Find executives** on the **In universe** toolbar fills the position with people for you.

- Tick the companies you want searched, or tick none and Uncava takes the first companies that have nobody mapped yet.
- Before it starts, Uncava says which companies it will search and how many profiles it may take from each. Press **Find executives** again to go ahead.
- It reads the brief to decide which titles to look for, searches each company, and adds the best matches as executives.
- A strip under the toolbar shows progress. You can keep working while it runs, and only one run goes at a time per position.

Executives it adds are marked **Sourced** in their drawer. Check them like any other: they're suggestions, not decisions.
