A position's **Outreach** page is one place to work every executive you are approaching: who is in conversation, everything that happened with them, and the email you write back.

- **Conversations** on the left lists everyone your team is in conversation with on this position, newest first. **To do** narrows it to who needs you: a reply to answer, a follow-up due, someone to prepare before a first email, or a delivery problem such as a bounce. **Scheduled** and **Sent** show emails waiting to go and emails that went. **Team** shows everyone's; **Mine** shows your own.
- **Their conversation** in the middle shows every email sent and received, sequence steps, calls, notes and meetings on this position, newest first. On a wide screen the top one opens by itself.
- **Write** at the foot: reply in their thread, or start a first email. Send now or schedule it, and attach one of their documents or a file of your own. Sending a reply closes their reply task.
- **New email** at the top writes to anyone on the position: type a name, a title or a company. **Sequences**, under Outreach in the side menu beside **Conversations**, lists your sequences and everyone in them.
- **Their details** on the right show their stage, contact details, key dates and current sequence. **Open persistent candidate record** opens everything else.

The page updates by itself as replies, emails and tasks arrive. Notes and emails are for your team only, never for a client.
