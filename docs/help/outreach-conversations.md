A position's **Outreach** page is one place to work every executive you are approaching: who needs you next, everything that happened with them, and the email you write back.

- **The action queue** on the left lists who needs action: a reply to answer, a follow-up due, someone to prepare before a first email, or something that went wrong, such as a bounce. **Mine** shows your own; **Team** shows everyone's.
- **Their conversation** in the middle shows every email sent and received, sequence steps, calls, notes and meetings on this position, newest first.
- **Write** at the foot: reply in their thread, or start a first email. Send now or schedule it, and attach one of their documents or a file of your own. Sending a reply closes their reply task.
- **Their details** on the right show their stage, contact details, key dates and current sequence. **Open persistent candidate record** opens everything else.

The page updates by itself as replies, emails and tasks arrive. Notes and emails are for your team only, never for a client.
